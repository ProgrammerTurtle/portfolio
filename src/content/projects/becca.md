My L1 certification rocket (Big Engine Certification Completion Attempt). It's 5 feet tall, flies on an Aerotech I500, and is aiming for Mach 0.8 and about a mile up. Dual-deploy parachutes bring it down, and a GPS tracker I built from a Particle Boron and an Adafruit Ultimate GPS helps me find it.
