A cartesian toolhead where the cooling duct and heatsink are one piece of SLM-printed aluminum, shaped with airflow in mind. It uses an Orbiter 1.5 extruder and a BTT Eddy Duo Z probe. I designed it in under 48 hours for Hack Club Rework.
